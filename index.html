About me

Hello

I'm Tanya — a 3D artist and motion designer running the studio Dragon Dreams Digital.

I create 3D visuals and animation for brands and digital products: brand visuals, ad and promo animation, product visualization and abstract 3D art.

Inspiration comes from the ever-changing world of advertising — a combination of styles with seemingly incompatible elements fills each work with captivating color. I work in Cinema 4D and Redshift and love making colors with light rather than textures.

My process is built on clear communication, storyboarding and previs — so each project feels intentional from first sketch to final render.
